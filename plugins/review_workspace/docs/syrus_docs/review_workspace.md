# Review workspace

The Review workspace is a focused list of Jobs on which the signed-in operator can make an approval decision now. Enable the bundled **Review Workspace** plugin to add **Review** to the primary sidebar. Its badge is the number of currently actionable Jobs. When the Jobs dashboard is available, it also shows an optional notice with the actionable count and a link into Review; the Review route and sidebar entry remain available independently of the dashboard.

A Job appears only when it is visible and writable by the operator, is an ordinary non-investigation Job in the implemented state, can accept that operator's approval, and has no approval-blocking runtime work. Jobs with unresolved dependencies remain reviewable but sort after otherwise equivalent unblocked Jobs. Explicit priority, the time the Job became ready, and its numeric ID complete the deterministic ordering.

Selecting **Review** opens a full-screen, distraction-free workspace with the eligible Job rail, the canonical Job review evidence and source-diff experience, and approval and feedback actions. Visual evidence, diff versions, annotations, and plugin-contributed review content are the same content shown on Job detail. Jobs without screenshots show the canonical no-evidence state instead of a placeholder artifact.

The workspace stores no inbox, read/unread, or durable deferred state. Defer only removes a Job for the current workspace session. A deep link selects a Job only while it remains eligible; otherwise the workspace safely selects the first current item or shows its caught-up state. Approval and feedback use the normal Job lifecycle endpoints and recheck authorization and the current PR state. Successful actions remove the item and advance the stack.

On mobile, one Job is presented at a time. Swipe vertically on the Job to move through the stack, swipe right to open (but not complete) approval confirmation, swipe left to defer for the session, or long-press for details. The mobile card shows a compact decision brief built only from signals present on the Job (readiness, change scope, checks, review findings, test plan, visual evidence count, Epic), never inferring risk or change type, plus links to the Job, its Epic, its PR (opens GitHub in a new tab), and the full diff. A dismissible first-use gesture coach appears once; reopen it from the Job details sheet. Screenshot gestures remain inside the canonical evidence carousel, and gestures beginning at the browser edge do not claim rightward navigation. Visible controls and keyboard controls mirror every gesture: J/K selects Jobs, left/right navigates visual evidence, A starts approval, F opens feedback, and Escape exits. Reduced-motion preferences disable gesture transforms. Exiting returns to the previous application view.

Disabling the plugin removes its routes, sidebar entry, badge, and frontend contribution without changing Jobs or approval records.
